About

Parikshita Mathur is a disputes lawyer who pairs courtroom experience with a forensic approach to evidence.

Over 12 years she has built a practice across litigation, investigations and forensics, including appearances before the Delhi High Court and the Supreme Court of India. Her work today is mainly corporate litigation and corporate dispute resolution.

She is Group Legal Head at Caparo India and a Director of Ayatti by Caparo. She holds an LL.M. and is a Certified Fraud Examiner, which shapes how she handles white-collar crime: identify the issue early, build the record carefully, and act on the evidence.
